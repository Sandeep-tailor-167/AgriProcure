Frequently asked questions

What should I bring to the procurement centre?

Bring your digital token, valid photo ID, bank account details, and produce prepared according to the centre’s published requirements.

Can I reschedule or cancel a booking?

You can cancel a confirmed booking before check-in. Rescheduling is handled by cancelling the current slot and selecting a new available slot.

Is the queue waiting time guaranteed?

No. It is a range based on recent service times. Weather, inspections, or centre interruptions can change it.

Does AgriProcure send or process my payment?

No. AgriProcure displays only payment updates verified by an authorised source; it does not perform banking operations.
